How this template works across tenants
  • One shared Next.js template renders every school's public site; per-tenant data (name, logo, colors, copy, stats, news, gallery, contact info) is pulled by school_id at request time — same layout, different content, same as this toggle swaps Gambia ↔ Zimbabwe.
  • The News section reads from the same admin-published feed that powers the alumni portal (per the alumni module spec) — the school posts once, it surfaces on both the public site and alumni view.
  • Hero and About sections are the highest-trust surfaces for first-time visitors — this is where "not profit-driven" framing matters most; keep copy institutional, not sales-oriented.
  • Contact section doubles as the entry point to the V2 enquiry form (not built yet) — for now it's a simple message box.
  • Performance budget from the design doc applies here too: hero image should be the only large asset above the fold, WebP, under 500KB.
